How do I pay?

By card through secure checkout. If online checkout isn’t open for a spot yet, the button opens a short request instead and we email you a secure payment link within 1 business day. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

Is a basic listing really free?

Yes, always. Every market belongs in the directory; paid spots are optional extras and are always labelled.

When does my spot go live?

Within 24 hours of payment, after we check your event against your own page. We email you when it’s up.

What if my event is cancelled?

If it’s cancelled before your featured week starts, you get a full refund or can move the week.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes, any time. It stops at the end of the month you paid for.

Do you accept every sponsor?

No. We only take sponsors that are useful to vendors, and we can decline any placement. See the terms.
