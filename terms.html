Terms of use

Jax Vendor Events (“we”, “us”) is a free directory and weekly email of vendor markets, craft fairs, pop-ups and festivals in Northeast Florida (Duval, St. Johns, Clay, Nassau, Baker, Flagler and Putnam counties). By using the site, signing up for the email or sending us a listing, you agree to these terms.

  • Free to browse. Browsing, saving events and the weekly email are free.
  • We are not the organizer. We don't run the events we list, take vendor applications or collect booth fees. Your agreement for any event is with its organizer.
  • Submissions. When you send us an event, profile, logo or photo, you confirm you're allowed to share it and you let us show it on the site and in the email. We may edit for length and clarity, and we can decline or remove any listing or profile.
  • Fair use of the site. Don't scrape the directory in bulk, post false or misleading events, or use the forms to send spam.
  • No warranty. The site and email are provided “as is”. To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses from relying on a listing, including booth fees, travel or lost sales.
  • Florida law. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Florida. We may update them; the date above shows the latest version.

Listing accuracy

Always confirm the date, fee, rules and application with the organizer before you pay or travel. Events change, sell out and get cancelled.
  • We check listings against each organizer's own page, a city calendar or a public event post. A Checked badge shows the date we last read that page.
  • Unverified means we found the event in a public post but haven't confirmed this year's details yet.
  • Dates for weekly or monthly markets are projected from the published schedule.
  • Spotted a mistake? Tell us through List your event and we'll fix it.

Paid placements

  • Featured listings, deadline spotlights and sponsorships are always labelled “Featured” or “Sponsor”. Paying never changes whether an event is listed or how it is checked.
  • Prices are on the advertise page. You pay by secure card checkout (Stripe) or by a secure payment link we email you; we never ask for card details in our own forms and never see your full card number.
  • Monthly plans renew each month until you cancel; cancel any time and the plan stops at the end of the month you paid for.
  • If an event is cancelled before its featured week starts, we refund in full or move the week. Once a placement has run, it isn't refundable.
  • We only accept sponsors that are useful to vendors. We don't accept ads for anything illegal, adult, gambling, weapons or “get rich” schemes.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Weekly email: your email address (and first name if you give it).
  • Forms: what you type into List your event, Get featured or a profile, including your name and email.
  • On your device only: saved events, your “My matches” settings and theme are stored in your browser. We don't receive them.

How we use it

  • To send the weekly email, reply to you, check and publish your listing, and send payment links you asked for.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Services we use

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.
  • Forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.
  • From launch, the weekly email is sent through an email newsletter service, which records opens and clicks so we can tell what's useful. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Payments, when you buy a placement, are handled by the payment provider; we never see your full card number.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time with the link in any email.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information or profile and we'll do it within 30 days.
  • The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your information: use the contact form. A direct email address goes up with the site's own domain at launch.
