Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is month to month with no contract. Cancel whenever you like and it stops at the end of the month you paid for. Just reply to any of our emails or your receipt.

How am I billed?

$6 a month by card, through Stripe’s secure checkout. We never see or store your card number.

When does it start working?

Right away. Pro attaches to your account automatically after checkout, and every listing opens up.

Does it cover all of Florida?

Yes: every region we list, from the Panhandle to the Keys, including campus markets. New markets are added every day.

Do I need an account first?

Yes, a free one. It takes a minute, and it’s how your Pro unlocks on every device you log in on.
